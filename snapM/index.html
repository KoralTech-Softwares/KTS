What is SnapM?

SnapM is an intelligent media management app built to organize, auto-save, and manage your captured content. Whether it is photos, HD videos, documents, or voice notes, SnapM keeps everything structured, synced to the cloud, and automatically deletes expired files to save space.

⚡

Key Features

📷

Smart Media Capture

Capture photos, HD videos, multi-page documents, and voice notes with automatic folder categorization.

⏱

Auto-Expiry & Space Cleanup

Set custom expiration durations for transient media. Expired items auto-delete to keep your phone storage clean.

☁

Cloud Sync & Backup

Back up your files to Google Drive securely with seamless automated background synchronization.

▣

Custom Directory Storage

Select local folders on your device to save all captured photos, videos, audio, and PDF documents.

⌕

Instant Search & File Groups

Organize files into custom folders, filter by media category, and search instantly by name or tag.
