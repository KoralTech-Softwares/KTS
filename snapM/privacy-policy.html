Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

SnapM gives utmost importance to your privacy. Please take a minute to review the details of our privacy practices below.

Required Access Permissions:

  • To be able to take pictures and record audio and videos, we need access to your camera and microphone.
  • To be able to store and display pictures, videos, and documents, we need access to your photos, media, and files.
  • To provide backup and synchronization functionality, SnapM may use Android foreground data synchronization services.

What Information We Collect:

SnapM does not collect your photos, videos, audio recordings, or documents for its own use. Content stored locally remains on your phone, and you have control over sharing it with other applications or services.

If you choose to use SnapM's optional Google Drive backup feature, the selected photos, videos, documents, and supported application data will be uploaded to your Google Drive account for backup and synchronization. This transfer occurs only when you choose to use the backup or synchronization feature.

Google Drive Backup:

SnapM integrates with Google Drive to provide optional backup and synchronization. When you enable this feature, selected content is transferred to your Google Drive account and stored there according to your Google Drive settings.

SnapM does not sell your backed-up content or use it for advertising purposes. Your use of Google Drive is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Sharing:

SnapM does not share your photos, videos, audio recordings, or documents with third parties for advertising or other unrelated purposes. You may choose to share your content using third-party applications installed on your device.

When you voluntarily share content with another application or service, that application's or service's privacy policy will apply to the content you share.

Changes:

Our Privacy Policy may change occasionally. We will not reduce your rights under this Privacy Policy without providing appropriate, advance notice where required. We will post any Privacy Policy changes on this page and, in the event of significant changes, may provide additional notice through the application or other appropriate means.

Contact Us:

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact us through our developer account email on Google Play.
